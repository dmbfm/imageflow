
ImageFlow is an image editor with a node-based interface where you use nonlinear, non destructive workflows to adjust photos, create your own designs, apply artistic filters and effects to images, and more!

With ImageFlow you can combine more than 200 different nodes to touch up your photos, modify your images, or create complete designs from scratch! Nodes are non-destructive and can be combined non-linearly. This means you can go back and modify and step in your edit at any time you want.

【Core Features】
- Node-based interface with more than 200 nodes.
- Preview each node output in a floating panel, a docked panel or in a separate window.
- Import images via file dialog, drag-and-drop, pasting from the clipboard or from your Photos library. Supported formats include: png, jpeg, gif, tiff, RAW, ktx, avif, heic, heif, pbm, and more!  
- Export as a file to png, jpeg, tiff or heic, or just copy to the clipboard and paste anywhere.

【A Universe of Nodes】
- More than 200 nodes in total.
- 10+ blur nodes.
- 20+ color adjustment nodes.
- 30+ color effects and filters.
- Composite operations.
- Distortion and transforms.
- 10+ procedural image generation nodes, including gradient generators.
- 20+ stylized filter nodes.
- Vector and math functions and operations.
- Basic visual programming capabilities.
